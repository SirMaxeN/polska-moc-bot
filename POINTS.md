# Clan-war scoring evidence

The user's 16 screenshots are current and authoritative for their server. Displayed points include current PolskaMoc tech; conflicting internet tables do not replace them.

Provisional formula: base × (1 + action_bonus/100 + day_bonus/100). Additive bonuses and rounding are not independently confirmed. Days 1–5: 40%, day 6: 0%. Forging, research and forge spending action bonus: 0%; other categories: 40%. Examples: 125×1.8=225, 3000×1.8=5400, 920×1.4=1288. Multiplicative bonuses would produce 1.96.
